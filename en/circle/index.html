Circle lock in five steps

Freeze the circle at the size you want, throw while the Pokémon is attacking, and hit the same size every time.

1
Hold the ball to shrink the circle, then let goJust holding shrinks it; no need to spin. Let go at the size you want: the circle disappears but stays at that size.
2
Wait for it to attackThrow while it's still and it may start attacking mid-flight and knock the ball away. Waiting is half the trick.
3
When it starts, grab the ballThe circle doesn't shrink while it attacks. But if you keep holding after the attack ends, it shrinks again and resets to the big circle, so be ready to throw as soon as you grab.
4
Throw 1 second before it endsThe ball takes about 1 second to fly. If you wait to see the attack end, you're too late. The Pokémon has to be still when the ball arrives, not when you throw, so watch closely how the attack ends.
5
Nice → Great → ExcellentAim for the small circle from the start and you won't learn the timing. Get the timing down with a big circle first, then shrink it.

How this differs from the game

Each Pokémon's attack differs in length and shape, and Shadow Pokémon attack much more often. Here you only practice normal/Shadow and short/long attacks, so in the game, watch how your target's attack ends once or twice first. Curveballs aren't included: straight throws are enough to learn the timing.

Circle lock, until it's in your fingers

Watching a video doesn't make your hands follow, so this lets you practice right here. Start at Nice; three hits in a row moves you up a level. No Poké Balls wasted.
