What to know before you stack

Each one has a catch beyond its size.

×2Party Power
The biggest, but it applies only to your next Charged Attack. It doesn't trigger by itself: fill the gauge with Fast Attacks, then tap the blue circle at the bottom left.
+30%Mega
The biggest always-on bonus, but it goes to the other Trainers fighting with you, not you. Even moves of a different type than the Mega get +10%. If several people bring one, only the highest applies.
+20%Weather
Free, but it boosts the boss's moves too. If the weather boosts the boss's move type, you faint faster.
2.56Double weakness
In Pokémon GO a weakness is 1.6×, not 2×, and a double weakness is 2.56×, not 4×. There is no 0× either: the least effective case is 0.244×.

Same move, 1.6× or 5.3×

Hitting a weakness alone gives 1.6×; stack same-type bonus, weather, friends and Mega and the same move lands at 5.3×. Turn the switches on one by one to see what is big and what is a trap. Only multipliers checked against sources for the video are included.
