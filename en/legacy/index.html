Good to know

Added when you evolve
Most legacy moves are added the moment you evolve. Even if you have a Rhyhorn, Rock Wrecker only appears once it becomes Rhyperior. Evolve after the event ends and you won't get it.
Four kinds of TMs
Your bag holds Fast TM · Charged TM · Elite Fast TM · Elite Charged TM separately. Legacy moves can only be learned with the “Elite” ones: Elite Fast TM for Fast Attacks, Elite Charged TM for Charged Attacks.
Trading keeps the moves
When you trade, only the IVs are rerolled — the moves stay. So for a shiny with disappointing IVs, it pays to evolve it during the event and lock in the legacy move.
When it was last available
For moves handed out through events like Community Day and the year-end recap Community Days, the last date is listed. The older it is, the longer the wait for a return. Moves that were already legacy before June 2021 are left over from old move rebalances, not events, so they have no date.
Check it in the Pokédex
Pokédex → the Pokémon → at the bottom of the BATTLE tab there's an “ELITE TM ATTACKS” section, with power listed too.

What does it learn when it evolves?

Moves that can only be learned with Elite TMs, grouped by evolution line. Search for Rhyhorn and you'll also see that Rhyperior learns Rock Wrecker. Power and energy are exactly as written in the game data.
