Your search string appears right below as you choose. Commas, exclamation marks and dashes are handled for you.

Appraisal to shownone chosen = all
Excludeeach gets its own exclamation mark
Evolution conditionsall chosen must match
CP · days since caughtone side is enough

Days 0 = today (within 24 hours). Max CP 1500 = Great League, 2500 = Ultra League.

Year caughtone only
Distance from where caughtdash after the number = or more

Pokémon caught in remote raids are measured from the raid Gym. For distance searches, also exclude legendary to make it easier.
