Is the shop's "46% OFF" real?

The crossed-out "original price" in the shop is what the items cost if you bought them one by one. But a 3-pack of Premium Battle Passes works out to 83 coins each, so the real discount against bundles is smaller. Enter the box price and contents to see the real discount, the cost of one raid, and how it compares with the cheapest it has ever been.
