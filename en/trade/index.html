Good to know

Text under slots
Pokémon with moves they can't learn now, like Spacial Rend or Roar of Time, are worth more. Tap a slot and write the move under it so it stands out. No need to write IVs — they are rerolled when you trade.
A picture doesn't mean it's released
The shinies here are every shiny that has a picture in the game files. Pictures sometimes arrive weeks before an event, and some are in the files but not released yet.
Backgrounds it has appeared with
A green tag means there is a record of that Pokémon appearing with that background. The records don't say which costume, so it's shown per species. "Base only" backgrounds get extra effects like moons or patterns in the game, so they look simpler here than on screen.
Your list stays on this device
Your list is saved only in this browser and never sent to a server. On another device you'll need to make it again.

Looking for · Can give on one card

Instead of writing a long post when you look for trades, show it in one picture. Pick shinies and costumes, add backgrounds to the ones that have them, and write legacy moves under the slots. Korean backgrounds like Seoul, Incheon and Jeju are included too.
